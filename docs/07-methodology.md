# Methodology

> Part of [Colour Research](../README.md).

## How the reviews were produced

1. **Question first.** Each review starts from a practical question, such as why copper-diamond interfaces limit conductivity, or why hydrogen recycling of magnets is hard.
2. **Search.** We searched public sources: peer-reviewed journals and review articles, patents, standards bodies, government and agency reports (for example the International Energy Agency), manufacturer announcements, and technical blogs for pointers.
3. **Select.** We favoured recent reviews and primary papers, and noted which claims came only from manufacturers or secondary sources.
4. **Extract.** We recorded numbers with conditions (temperature, particle size, method) and what each source did and did not show.
5. **Synthesise.** We wrote plain-language summaries, separating what is established from what is reported or hypothesised.
6. **Check.** We re-read claims against the sources and flagged anything we could not verify.

## What this is not

- It is not a systematic review, meta-analysis or original experiment.
- It does not replace the original papers, standards or professional advice.

## Reproducing this work

Every reference is listed with a link where one exists. If you cannot reproduce a claim from its source, open a *Correction* issue.

## Updating

We revise documents when new evidence appears or errors are found. Each meaningful change is recorded in the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md).

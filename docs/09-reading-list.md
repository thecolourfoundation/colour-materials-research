## Copper-diamond composites

- Xue et al. (2025), interface optimization and preparation of diamond-copper composites, *Frontiers in Materials*: https://frontiersin.org/journals/materials/articles/10.3389/fmats.2025.1582990/full
- Review of interface thermal conductance in copper-diamond composites, *Journal of Materials Science* (2020): https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10853-020-05443-3
- Abyzov et al. (2015), W-coated diamond in copper, *Materials and Design* 76: https://peeref.com/works/1029412
- Cui et al., Cr-coated diamond and copper by spark plasma sintering: https://journal.hep.com.cn/ijmmm/EN/10.1007/s12613-016-1285-1
- NASA report on diamond-composite fabrication challenges: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20160001827/downloads/20160001827.pdf

## NdFeB magnets and recycling

- Habibzadeh, Kucuker, Gokelma (2023), *ACS Omega* 8, 17431-17445: https://doi.org/10.1021/acsomega.3c00299
- He et al. (2021), grain boundary diffusion sources, *Metals* 11, 1434
- Tian et al. (2025), heavy rare earth grain boundary diffusion, *Materials Reports*: https://www.mater-rep.com/EN/Y2025/V39/I6/24040174
- Burkhardt (2024), hydrogen assisted recycling of Nd-Fe-B magnets, a critical review, SIPS 2024

## Supply and policy

- International Energy Agency, *Rare earth elements*: https://www.iea.org/reports/rare-earth-elements/executive-summary

## Standards to look up

- ASTM E1461 (thermal diffusivity by the flash method) and ASTM E1269 (specific heat by DSC). Confirm current versions before use.

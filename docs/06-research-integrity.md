# Research integrity

> Part of [Colour Research](../README.md).

This policy explains how we try to keep this work honest, especially because Colour has a commercial interest in the materials it discusses.

## Evidence labels

| Label | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Established** | Standard textbook science or widely replicated results | Fourier's law of heat conduction |
| **Reported** | A specific result from a named source that we have not reproduced | A manufacturer's stated conductivity for a composite |
| **Hypothesis** | A reasoned proposal we intend to test | That a particular interface layer improves repeatability |
| **Unknown** | We could not find evidence either way | Behaviour in a specific module geometry |

Unlabelled statements should be read as Reported. Check the cited source.

## Rules we follow

1. **Source every number.** State the temperature, conditions and method behind it.
2. **Prefer primary sources.** Papers, standards, patents and official reports come before blogs and press releases. Manufacturer claims are marked as such.
3. **Separate targets from results.** A target is never written as an achievement.
4. **Show baselines.** Performance claims are given next to a comparison.
5. **Report negatives.** When we have our own data, misses and failures are published with successes.
6. **State uncertainty.** Numbers from different papers are not directly comparable, and we say so.
7. **Disclose interests.** See [governance](../GOVERNANCE.md).

## Known limitations of this work

- These are narrative reviews, not systematic reviews. We did not search every database or screen every paper.
- Much of the source material was found through public web search. Paywalled material is under-represented.
- Some values are textbook approximations and are marked General knowledge.
- We are early, and we will have made mistakes. Please tell us.

## Corrections

Errors are fixed, recorded in the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md), and credited. Material errors are flagged visibly.

## Safety

This is educational material. Hazardous processes (fine metal powders, hydrogen, high-temperature furnaces, plating chemicals) must only be carried out by trained people in suitable facilities.

# Frequently asked questions

> Part of [Colour Research](../README.md).

**Is Colour selling a product?**
Not yet. Colour is early-stage and pre-experimental. This repository is research and teaching material.

**Do you have samples or measured data?**
Not yet. We will publish data, with methods and uncertainty, when we have it.

**Is this peer reviewed?**
No. These are our own literature syntheses. We invite expert review and we credit it.

**Why two programmes?**
Both are materials problems tied to power and heat in demanding hardware, and both reward careful process control and measurement. Copper-diamond composites are the first focus.

**Why are some numbers marked "General knowledge"?**
They are standard textbook values we did not trace to a single paper. Treat them as approximate.

**Can I reuse this material?**
Yes, with credit, under CC BY 4.0. See [LICENSE-CONTENT.md](../LICENSE-CONTENT.md).

**How do I report an error?**
Open a *Correction* issue and include a source.

**What do you not publish?**
Customer data and specifications, confidential partner information, and proprietary process details.

**Can I follow the processes described to make these materials myself?**
These documents are educational summaries, not procedures. Fine powders, hydrogen, furnaces and plating chemicals are hazardous and require training and proper facilities.

**How do I work with Colour?**
Email buildwithcolours@gmail.com.

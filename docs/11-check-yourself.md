# Check yourself

> Part of [Colour Research](../README.md). Educational material. Numbers are approximate textbook or literature values unless sourced. See [research integrity](06-research-integrity.md).

Test your understanding of the primers. Cover the right column and answer first.

| Question | Short answer |
|---|---|
| Why does diamond conduct heat so well? | Light atoms, stiff bonds and a regular lattice let phonons travel far without scattering |
| Why is the interface the main problem? | Copper does not wet diamond, so poor bonding adds thermal resistance at each particle |
| Why add a carbide former, and what is the risk? | It bonds diamond to copper, but too much builds a thick layer that blocks heat |
| What is graphitization? | Diamond turning to graphite at high temperature, which hurts conductivity |
| Why does CTE matching matter? | Mismatch with the chip causes stress and cracks during thermal cycling |
| How does a laser flash give conductivity? | It measures diffusivity, and you multiply by density and specific heat |
| Why use pressure-assisted sintering? | Full density at lower temperature and shorter time |
| What is DoE for? | Separating the effects of several variables with few runs |
| What is the valley of death? | The gap between a lab result and a qualified, funded product |
| Why can qualification slow you down? | Customers run long reliability tests before accepting a new material |

# Governance

## Roles

- **Maintainer:** Malik (Founder, Colour). Decides what is merged, keeps the changelog, and is accountable for the quality of the work.
- **Contributors:** anyone who proposes a correction, reference or addition.
- **Reviewers:** domain experts who agree to read a document critically. We credit them with permission.

## Decisions

Changes are merged when they are accurate, sourced, in scope and clearly written. The maintainer decides, and explains the reasoning in the pull request or issue when declining. Disagreements about evidence are settled by sources, not seniority.

## Disclosure of interest

Colour is a company with commercial interests in the materials discussed here. To limit bias:

1. Every factual claim needs a source or an evidence label.
2. We state limits and negative findings as clearly as positive ones.
3. We name patents and competitors that constrain our plans.
4. We accept and credit corrections, including ones that go against us.

## Corrections

If an error is found, we fix it, record it in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md), and credit the reporter unless they prefer otherwise. Material errors are marked clearly, not quietly removed.

## What we publish and what we keep private

We publish literature syntheses, teaching material, open questions, methods and, when we have it, our own results. We do not publish customer data, customer specifications, confidential partner information or proprietary process details. See the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md).

## Changes to governance

Proposals to change this document are made by pull request and decided by the maintainer after discussion.

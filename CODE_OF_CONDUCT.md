# Code of Conduct

Inspired by the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).

## Our standard

This is a technical community. We want rigorous, direct, respectful discussion where people can disagree about evidence without disrespecting each other.

**Expected:**
- Critique claims and methods, not people.
- Say what you know, what you don't, and how sure you are.
- Credit others' work and corrections.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding bad faith.

**Not acceptable:**
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or threats.
- Doxxing or sharing others' private information.
- Spam, promotion unrelated to the work, or misleading claims about this project.
- Sharing confidential or proprietary information that is not yours to share.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone represents this project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and restrict participation, to enforce this code. Report concerns to buildwithcolours@gmail.com with the subject "Conduct". We will handle reports confidentially and fairly.

# Contributing

Thank you for helping make this work more accurate. The most valuable contributions are corrections, missing references, and critical review from people who know the field.

## Ways to help

1. **Correct an error.** Open a *Correction* issue with the location, what is wrong, and a source.
2. **Add a reference.** Propose a paper, standard or dataset, with a sentence on what it adds.
3. **Review a document.** Open a *Technical review* issue and tell us what you checked and what concerns you.
4. **Improve clarity.** Pull requests that make explanations simpler without losing accuracy are welcome.

## Standards for contributions

- **Source every factual claim.** Prefer primary sources (peer-reviewed papers, standards, patents, official reports) over blogs and press releases. If you only have a secondary source, say so.
- **Use evidence labels** (Established, Reported, Hypothesis, Unknown) as described in [research integrity](docs/06-research-integrity.md).
- **Use SI units** and state temperatures, conditions and measurement methods with any number.
- **Do not paste copyrighted text.** Paraphrase, and quote only short passages with attribution.
- **No confidential information.** Do not contribute material that belongs to your employer or a client.

## Pull request process

1. Fork the repository and create a branch.
2. Make a focused change, and update the changelog if the change is meaningful.
3. Open a pull request using the template. Explain what changed and why, with sources.
4. A maintainer will review. We may ask for sources, wording changes or a narrower scope.

## What we will not merge

- Unsourced performance claims or marketing language.
- Hazardous procedures presented as instructions.
- Customer-confidential or personal data.
- Attacks on individuals or companies.

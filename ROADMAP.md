# Roadmap

This is a statement of intent, not a promise. Dates move, and plans change when evidence does.

## Published now

- Primers on heat transport, interfaces, processing routes, measurement and qualification
- A literature review and white paper on high-temperature magnets
- Customer specification-sheet templates
- Research integrity policy, methodology and glossary

## Planned next

- A literature review of copper-diamond composites for power-module baseplates and heat spreaders
- A methods note on thermal characterisation of high-diffusivity composites
- A catalogue of open datasets and tools useful for composite and magnet research
- A public errata log

## Published only when we have it

- Our own measured data, with methods, uncertainties and negative results
- Reproducible protocols for tests we have run
- Independent verification reports, where our partners permit

## Not planned

- Customer data, customer specifications or partner-confidential information
- Proprietary process recipes, models or run databases
- Marketing claims presented as research

## How to influence this

Open an issue with a question you want answered or a reference we missed.
